At a glance

Static design vs motion stack — different success metric, different course.

Why not fold this into Design?

  • · Different success metric: rendered MP4 vs openable PPTX
  • · Different time cost: renders and iteration loops
  • · Different stack: HyperFrames / Remotion vs DESIGN.md + stills

Planned outline

A

Motion mental model

Timeline vs slide deck. Why iteration costs minutes, not seconds. When not to use video.

B

HyperFrames path (default)

HTML-native compositions on Hermes. Script → scenes → captions → MP4. One short piece end-to-end.

C

Stills into motion

Import Design 01 / Imagine keyframes. Storyboard before render. Kill weak frames early.

D

Remotion path (advanced)

React compositions and agent skills when you need code-level ownership and reuse.

E

Studio habits

Render budgets, naming, review before publish. Multi-agent video pipelines later — not day one.
